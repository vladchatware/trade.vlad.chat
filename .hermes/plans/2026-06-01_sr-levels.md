## Architecture

Detection is pure functions operating on `Candle[]`. A React hook runs them on the candle stream. A lightweight-charts primitive draws horizontal lines.

```
Candle[] → findSwings() → clusterLevels() → SRLevel[]
                                ↓
                        SRPrimitive draws lines
```
